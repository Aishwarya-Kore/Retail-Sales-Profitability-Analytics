# Retail Sales & Profitability Analytics

## 1. Project Overview

This project analyses retail sales data to evaluate business performance, understand profitability, identify customer and product trends, and generate actionable business insights.

It follows an end-to-end data analytics workflow, from data cleaning and exploration to SQL analysis, Python-based EDA and interactive Power BI dashboard development.

## 2. Business Objectives

- Analyse overall sales, profit, and profitability.
- Identify monthly sales trends and month-over-month growth.
- Evaluate category and sub-category performance.
- Identify top-performing and loss-making products.
- Understand customer purchasing behaviour.
- Analyse regional and state-level performance.
- Evaluate discount patterns and their relationship with profitability.
- Analyse shipping duration and shipping mode performance.
